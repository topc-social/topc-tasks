# How small co-working spaces make money

For topc-social/topc-tasks#57. Desk research, 2026-10-21. No small Pune operator has published a revenue split or a profit and loss, so this is pieced together from founder interviews (mostly 2015–2020), the listed chains, analysts and listing sites. Each source is marked: first-hand, news, analyst or vendor. Reddit, LinkedIn and YouTube couldn't be searched from here; several YourStory pages blocked full reads (marked "snippet").

## The model in plain words

It's real estate arbitrage. Lease a floor (₹50–100 per sq ft a month in Pune), fit it out (₹1,500–2,250 per sq ft), and resell it as seats at ₹6,000–12,000 a month each. Most of the money is monthly seats and small-team cabins. Virtual office / GST-address clients are pure margin but cheap and getting cheaper. Meeting rooms, day passes, events and the café are small extras. Breakeven is around 60% full; steady profit needs 75–85%. With a fixed lease the operator carries all the risk, which is why the chains moved to profit-sharing deals with landlords.

## Revenue

1. Memberships about 70% of revenue, events 10–15%, rest virtual offices (Stirring Minds, Delhi). CoworkIn: about 60% memberships, 20% team memberships, rest events. First-hand, 2015. [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/how-profitable-are-coworking-spaces-in-india/251056)
2. Virtual offices called the most profitable product, "barely any cost". Analyst opinion; Awfis doesn't break this out. [Substack](https://rtomar.substack.com/p/the-small-cap-coworking-leader-quietly)
3. Virtual office prices ₹583–849 a month on aggregators; a guide says ₹2,000–5,000. Vendor. [Qdesq](https://www.qdesq.com/virtual-office/delhi), [CoHub](https://cohubindia.com/virtual-office/bangalore), [firststartup](https://www.firststartup.in/blog/coworking-space-startups-india-cost-business-plan-profit-2026)
4. Early startups register at co-working spaces for statutory needs "like GST registration". Aggregator report, not neutral. [myHQ × Anarock](https://websitemedia.anarock.com/media/my_HQ_industry_report_46d8dd4228.pdf)
5. GST physical verification is the risk: mailbox-only addresses get rejected most. Practitioner/vendor. [CoFynd](https://cofynd.com/blog/does-gst-department-accept-virtual-office-as-registered-address), [CharteredHelp](https://charteredhelp.com/why-virtual-office-gst-registration-gets-rejected/)
6. Pune: StartHub KP day pass ₹600, meeting rooms from ₹850/hr; The Missing Link KP desks from ₹300. Listings. [CoworkingCafe](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/coworking-property/in/mh/pune/starthub-coworking-space), [myHQ](https://myhq.in/coworking-space/the-missing-link-koregaonpark)

## Customers

7. About half of Awfis's business is clients under 100 seats; they asked for waivers and cancelled in the 2020 lockdown. News/analyst. [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2020/04/covid-19-coworking-segment-lockdown)
8. Demand shifting "from freelancers and startups to corporate and enterprise clients". Aggregator report. [myHQ × Anarock](https://websitemedia.anarock.com/media/my_HQ_industry_report_46d8dd4228.pdf)
9. Smaller cities want cabins: S.PACE (Bhopal etc.) is 80% cabins, ₹3,000 per workstation to ₹60,000 per suite; first 16 units full in 15 days. First-hand, 2020, snippet. [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2020/11/startup-bharat-bhopal-based-coworking-startup-space)
10. The Office Pass (NCR): ~140 small-business clients at ~₹7,000/seat; fell to 45 clients and 22% occupancy in lockdown; breakeven ~60%. First-hand. [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/how-we-started-a-1-2m-year-coworking-business-from-india)

## Ownership and franchising

16. Awfis runs ~57–67% of centres on "managed aggregation": the landlord funds most of the fit-out and shares the profit. Revenue-share deals sometimes start with a minimum guarantee after 3–6 months. News/analyst. [Flex Insights](https://theflexinsights.com/awfis-eyes-tier-2-cities-and-managed-models-to-power-next-phase-of-growth/), [99acres](https://www.99acres.com/articles/how-new-deal-structures-are-aiding-the-growth-of-coworking-spaces-in-india.html)

[WorkLoft](https://workloft.in/franchise), [Franchise India](https://www.franchiseindia.com/brands/sierra-cartel-coworking-space.28854)

## What it means for TOPC House (inference)

- A fixed lease and the fit-out are what kill small operators. A profit share with an owner of an underused bungalow, and furnishing only a little, avoids both.
- No desks means no seat business. Money would come from memberships, day passes (Pune already charges ₹300–600), events and food.
- GST-address clients are the easy margin, but a residential bungalow may not pass verification. Check before counting on it.
- The Mesh shows a homely apartment format has worked in Pune at small scale. Whether it lasted is unknown.
