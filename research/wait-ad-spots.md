# Wait-screen ad spots: desk research memo

Task: topc-social/topc-tasks#67. Desk research, 2026-10-25.

Kickbacks (June 2026) turned Claude Code's loading spinner into ad space. Its weak point is that the waits run 30 seconds to minutes, so developers switch tabs and never see the line. This memo looks for spots where people wait with their eyes on a screen and keep watching.

I found 22 spots. Every number below has a source, and a cell says "no source found" where I found none. Most attention and dwell figures come from the companies that sell the ads, so read them as sales claims, not independent research.

## What keeps people watching

1. NN/g response-time limits: after 10 seconds, people turn to something else. The best spots have waits under about 10s or an end nobody can predict. [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/3-response-time-limits-interaction-design/)
2. Maister (1985): "Occupied time feels shorter than unoccupied time." That's theory, not proof that people look. [davidmaister.com](https://davidmaister.com/articles/5/52/index.html)
3. Namco's loading-screen patent (US 5718632) protected filling loading screens for 20 years, until 2015. [Techdirt](https://archive.techdirt.com/articles/20151127/06515332914/patent-mini-games-within-loading-screens-expires-explosion-better-game-loading-screens-forecasted.shtml)
4. How long people stay by each screen: lifts 5–15s, lobbies 4.5–12s, roadside 1.5–3s ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/418165/your-attention-please-out-of-hand-or-out-of-h.html)).
5. Shorter ads are watched through: 93% complete at 1–5s, 70% at 26–30s. [Mediavine](https://www.mediavine.com/?p=1205)

## Best picks for a small team with no money

1. **Vibe-coding build previews (Lovable, Bolt, v0).** Nobody sells this spot, the wait runs seconds to minutes, and eyes stay on the preview. It would be a browser extension, like Kickbacks in VS Code. Risk: those tools' terms may ban overlays (not checked).
2. **Clinic token screens in Pune.** The screen is already there and people watch it for their number. No ad network was found. Getting in costs time on the street, not money. Weak: there's no attention data at all.
3. **OTP, login and payment-pending screens in small Indian apps.** Waits of 5–30s with people staring at the phone, and nobody found selling them. Could be a free SDK, though Idlen.io already offers one.
4. **Free AI image tools.** Craiyon shows people put up with ads while an image generates. Small AI wrapper apps have waits nobody sells. Images fit better than video, whose waits are too long.
5. **Waiting rooms for small Indian event sellers.** People stay so they don't lose their place, and Queue-it only promotes the seller's own products. Weak: no attention data, and the big platforms run their own queues.

The coding-agent spinner itself is crowded: Kickbacks, IdleAds, Idlen and Sponsoric all launched around June 2026.

## Weak claims

- Every attention number (GSTV, Captivate, oOh!, Uber's "2 min") comes from the sellers themselves.
- The Indian prices are The Media Ant rack rates, not what deals close at.
- The spinner CPMs come from one newsletter; the OTP 30s figure comes from a vendor citing a study I couldn't find.
- The Lovable, Bolt and Sora times are anecdotes. The Volta shutdown comes from one op-ed. The Pune Metro report is undated.
- Seven spots (ATMs, OTP, clinics, UPI, metro, Wi-Fi, game loading) have no source for wait length.
- I didn't check the PhonePe rate card, or whether Lovable, Bolt and v0 allow overlays.
