## Call first

1. **Inscape Cowork**: the only place found that is a real bungalow with a garden and team rooms, rented by the day.
2. **The Voyage**: a café with a private room, tagged work friendly, open from 9am.
3. **Dario's Trattoria**: a private dining area for 10–40 in a quiet lane.
4. **Gyaan Adab Centre**: the right bungalow feel for creatives, and a phone number is listed.
5. **bombon**: a converted bungalow café whose upstairs might suit a group.

## Gaps for the scout

- No shoot-location bungalows were found online in these areas. Instagram (#shootlocationpune, #punelocations) and asking location agents is the way.
- Google Maps and Instagram weren't searched. Walking KP Lanes 5–7 and North Ave in Kalyani Nagar will turn up places that aren't listed anywhere.
- Searches that found nothing, so don't repeat them: "bungalow shoot location Pune Koregaon Park rent per day", "sceneloc8 Pune bungalow", "Pune small team offsite venue day bungalow 10 people Koregaon Park", "Kharadi OR Magarpatta cafe private room workshops".
