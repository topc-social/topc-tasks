# TOPC Neki ledger

TOPC's own project ledger: Neki earned for work on TOPC itself (the brand, the community, the co-op, its shared docs). Each project has its own ledger; a person's share of TOPC = their Neki here / all Neki here. Rules: TOPC's NEKI.md. Moved here from the private topc repo on 2026-10-09, unchanged.

Units: 1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at market price (what a competent mid-level freelancer in India would charge for the result). Rows are only ever added, never edited; corrections are new rows. Status is `provisional` until an outside judge reviews it, then `final`.

Kamal's past work on TOPC is row 1, so later rows are a real slice and not the whole.
