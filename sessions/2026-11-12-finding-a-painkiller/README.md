# Finding a painkiller (session, 2026-11-06 to 11-12)

Kamal, after two years with no traction: "I just went like hundred steps ahead to build a product that I think the world should have. Instead of making step zero or step one to build the product that the world needs right now." This session looked for that step zero.

## Decisions
- **Stop pitching Ideato to programmes as the startup** (11-11): "they alll want a stake". Ideato stays Kamal's own daily tool.
- **Apply with whatever fits each programme** (11-12): TOPC, one project or Kamal, matched on entity, stake, theme, traction and deadline. Stake deals are case by case. Logged in `ecosystem/TOPC.md`; the matched top 10 is on [#14](https://github.com/topc-social/topc-tasks/issues/14#issuecomment-6806356000).

## Findings
- **The 1% started with a stuck cap table.** At Kamal's first startup the sales head needed 7-8%, the partners wouldn't dilute, and 1% plus all the revenue cash wasn't enough. Research: free fixes exist (option pools, vesting) but only work before the fight, so it's weak as a product. Full memo: [key-hire-dilution.md](key-hire-dilution.md) ([#104](https://github.com/topc-social/topc-tasks/issues/104)).
- **"Entrepreneurship is hard" is a mission, not a problem.** The real root Kamal named: one person carrying everything, stressed, short of money, doubting. Ramu Kaka, Founderbot and Ideato all aim at that.
- **Painkiller test:** look for people already overpaying, queuing or duct-taping. Research across 21 cases: the most desperate markets (visa slots, Tatkal, medical seats) want queue-jumping or false promises, so they're out. The honest openings are earning businesses facing a deadline or shut-off: fixed-price rescue of vibe-coded apps, Amazon suspension appeals, DPDP packs. No buyer was asked. Full report: [where-people-overpay.md](where-people-overpay.md) ([#105](https://github.com/topc-social/topc-tasks/issues/105)).
- **Agent vaults and wallets:** vault pain is real (people move `.env` files and use containers) but [Infisical raised $40M](https://startwithidentity.com/blog/2026-10-27-infisical-raises-40-million-series-b-for-secrets-and-ai-agent-credentials.md) for it. Wallets are built by Stripe, Visa, Mastercard and Coinbase, with real spend [estimated under $30M a month](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/iosg-ai-agent-payment-infrastructure-enters-race-for-machine-economy-stripe).
- **Trust blocks public ideas.** A designer friend won't post ideas publicly because anyone can take them, and would start up only with someone she trusts. Same shape as earlier co-founder and friend feedback.
- **Facebook won on density, not exclusivity:** [over half of Harvard within a month](https://www.harvardmagazine.com/technology/harvard-facebook-history-mark-zuckerberg-winklevoss). "Startups" is a category, not a group. One Pune college was picked as the first tight group; not started.
- **Best pain signal so far: Kamal's dad.** He pays for Vyapar, hates it, and works around handwritten supplier notes. Idea: [snap-the-bill shop app](https://www.ideato.social/idea/403047e7-faef-4920-a499-443dbd788d59). Next step: test reading 5 of his supplier's notes.
