# "Apps you didn't know existed" accounts that could feature TOPC's products

Task: topc-social/topc-tasks#254. Follows the audience research (topc-tasks#250, `audience-from-zero.md`): small brands that grew borrowed someone else's audience first. Accounts that post "websites / apps / AI tools you didn't know about" lists are one way to borrow it. Nothing was sent, posted or paid for.

## What we found

- **Only one free route is stated anywhere: the Future Tools form** (Matt Wolfe, [futuretools.io/submit-a-tool](https://futuretools.io/submit-a-tool)). It's for AI tools; Matt reviews each submission. No fee on the form.
- **Most accounts state no route at all.** For them the way in is a DM or a business email, and whether a feature costs money is unknown until they answer. Many list creators pick sites themselves, so a good DM can still land free.
- **The bigger names sell slots:** Kevin Stratvert ($14k+ per Short), Jeff Su, AI Valley (Barsee), The AI Field, Unwind AI, Appscribed ($49 listing, unverified). TOPC spends nothing, so these are listed for completeness and ranked last.
- **India:** six Indian Instagram creators cover apps and websites; @manojsaru (Hindi, 3M) has a public business email. No large Indian creator was found doing this on YouTube, X or TikTok.
- **Formy has no public store listing yet** (its page is [formy-topc.netlify.app](https://formy-topc.netlify.app)). Pitching it before it's easy to install wastes the shot; pitch Chess Coach, WTF and Profiley first.

## How the numbers were read

- **Instagram:** follower strings read from each profile's preview tags on 2026-11-14 (spot-checked twice). Bios aren't readable that way, so emails and "DM for collab" lines come from Socialveins / Qoruz pages that quote the bios; marked as such.
- **YouTube:** subscriber counts from the channel page, 2026-11-14.
- **X:** counts from SuperX (superx.so), a third-party snapshot, read 2026-11-14. May be stale.
- **TikTok:** profiles don't load for scripts, so no TikTok count was read directly. TikTok counts are **unverified**.
- "Posts lists" is backed by the evidence link in each row (a list page, article or upload feed), not by counting reels.
