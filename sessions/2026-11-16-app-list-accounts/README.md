# Borrowing audiences: "apps you didn't know" creators (session, 2026-11-14 to 11-16)

TOPC has almost no audience (LinkedIn 92, Instagram 16). The audience research ([#250](https://github.com/topc-social/topc-tasks/issues/250)) found that small brands that grew borrowed someone else's audience first. Kamal noticed reels like "7 apps you didn't know about" and wanted TOPC's free products in front of those creators. Task: [#254](https://github.com/topc-social/topc-tasks/issues/254).

## Findings
- **26 accounts** across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and X, each with followers (dated, source named), niche, how to submit, free or paid, language and best-fit TOPC product. Full list and pitches: [app-list-accounts.md](app-list-accounts.md).
- **Only one free route is stated anywhere:** Matt Wolfe's [Future Tools form](https://futuretools.io/submit-a-tool). Most accounts state no route, so the way in is a DM, price unknown.
- **The big names sell slots.** Only Kevin Stratvert publishes prices: $14k+ per Short, $30k+ per video, $500+ for 2 weeks in his newsletter ([media kit](https://kevinstratvert.com/media-kit/)). Six more sell on request.
- **India:** 7 of the 26 are Indian creators; @manojsaru (Hindi, 3M) has a public business email.
- **Formy has no store listing yet**, so its pitches wait until it's easy to install.
